# Major School Offering Contract v001

## Purpose

Define the relationship between a major and universities.

The system must not answer major questions only from encyclopedia knowledge.

A major should connect to schools through a separate offering layer.

## Relationship

Major Identity

→ School Offering

→ Admission History

→ Experience Layer

## School Offering may contain

- school identifier
- major identifier
- training direction
- discipline characteristics
- regional relevance
- admission availability

## Separation

Knowledge layer answers:

- What is this major?
- What do students learn?
- What work can graduates do?

School offering answers:

- Which universities offer this major?
- What characteristics does the training have?

Admission layer answers:

- Scores
- Ranks
- Historical enrollment data

Experience layer answers:

- Student feedback
- Campus feelings

No layer replaces another.
