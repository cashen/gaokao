# Major Identity Contract v001

## Purpose

Define the canonical identity of every major before knowledge, school matching, and admission data are connected.

## Scope

This is not a single major article source. It is the identity layer for all undergraduate majors.

Each major identity should support:

- national major code
- standard name
- aliases
- category
- historical name mapping when applicable

Examples:

- 电气
- 电气工程
- 电气工程及其自动化

must resolve to the same major identity when they refer to the same catalog entry.

## Ownership

Major identity owns:

- normalization
- alias resolution
- stable identifiers

Major identity does not own:

- admission scores
- school rankings
- student comments

Those belong to their respective layers.
