# Major Knowledge Contract v001

## Ownership

`shared/resources/major/` is the only knowledge owner for major explanations.

## Required fields

Each major record must provide:

- id
- name
- category
- what_it_is
- university_learning
- core_courses
- graduation_paths
- employment_directions
- suitable_students
- attention_points

## Layer separation

Knowledge layer:

- professional facts
- learning content
- career directions

Experience layer:

- student comments
- campus feelings
- personal opinions

Admission layer:

- scores
- ranks
- historical data

These layers must not replace each other.

## Response order

For a major question:

1. Explain the major.
2. Explain university study.
3. Explain future work.
4. Explain employment directions.
5. Connect schools.
6. Connect historical admission data.
7. Add student experience separately.
