# Major Knowledge Resource Owner

This directory is the canonical owner for major knowledge shared by the site.

Consumers:

- AIPLuS
- Tongxue
- ln-rank

Rules:

- Do not create product-specific major explanations outside this owner.
- Experience data and student comments belong to experience layers, not knowledge layers.
- Historical admission data remains owned by admission data resources.

A major knowledge record explains the major itself before connecting schools, scores, or experiences.
